# The Harvester Ad Strategy (Evergreen Retargeting at Scale)

_Source: File 6 third section. The full retargeting ecosystem: Content Cycle Bin 1 (warm cold audiences 3-5 days, frequency 3-5), Direct Response Ads (days 5-30), Content Cycle Bin 2 (alongside DR ads), Trust Funnel (2-day messenger+email), Webinar retargeting by time-on-page, Sales page retargeting via 20+ buying-motive variations (Lamborghini-vs-protein-powder principle), 5-7 day interval retargeting up to 180 days, Content Cycle Bin 3 (customer retention days 5-180). Master-skill's 'retargeting' surface produces this — long-lived, evergreen, scaleable to \$1M+/month._

## THE HARVESTER AD STRATEGY

**Presented by:** Jeremy Haynes of Megalodon Marketing

### Why Is This Called 'The Harvester'?

"Most advertisers, marketers and entrepreneurs go straight for the close in their marketing efforts. They just pitch pitch and pitch to the same audience or a few different audiences hoping and praying they can convert 2% of the audiences they are targeting to barely profit.

If we compare those types of advertisers to farmers, they would be the types of farmers that go straight for the harvest, and would completely ignore the rest of harvest cycle, which includes key stages like planting, tending, watering, and letting the crops grow before harvesting, and managing multiple crop fields at one time to enable more profits are scale when harvesting.

You see, when you're a farmer who works at scale, you can run out of crops in one field if you're only harvesting all the time and ignoring the other parts of the harvesting cycle. The same goes with advertisers.

We can run out of customers to generate from a specific market if we are only trying to convert the ones are ready to make a transaction with our businesses. We need to be focused on the rest of the sales cycle too, which includes making people aware of us, then trusting us and finding commonality with our brands so we can convert them in shorter periods of time, but at the right time when they are later ready to transact with us.

I learned a key lesson the hard way at scale that lead me to creating this ad strategy out of a need for more customers to convert at scale that would remain an evergreen solution for heavy clients. Use this wisely, not for small advertisers with low budgets."

### Made For

Personal Brands, Companies, And Anyone with High Quality Social Media Content That Wants To Post and Maximize Reach On Multiple Social Media Channels, Including Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn and Youtube. This ad strategy is for those at scale, who want an evergreen ad strategy that can be scaled to millions in ad spend each month.

### Core Intention

"The intention of 'the Harvester' ad strategy, is to consistently enable the company using the strategy to have an evergreen 'field' of customers to harvest from. Most marketers and entrepreneurs go straight for the kill with direct response marketing, disrespecting everyone in the sales process.

In the game of direct response marketing, you're the equivalent of a mall sales person, just pitching anyone while crossing your fingers you're going to convert 2% of your audience. Meanwhile you're disrespecting and burning your ability to generate positive brand impressions with 98% of your targeted audience, just like you get in the mall when you're minding your business and someone comes up and starts pitching you on something you may not even be qualified for.

The harvester ad strategy is the modern day, evergreen ad strategy that you can deploy at scale to convert customers who are ready to convert, while generating new customers who build trust with your brand rapidly, later becoming available to harvest. All while spending ~100X less than your competitors who are leveraging direct response marketing.

The average cost of a video view, thru play, or cost per engagement is going to be around $.001-$.02 while your competition is going to be spending an average of $1.00 driving people to their website, meaning your dollar is literally going 100X-1000X further than their dollar."

### Why It Works

"Social interaction is now retargetable on Facebook, Instagram, Youtube and LinkedIn, meaning everyone who watches one of your videos, interacts with your pages or posts, messages you, saves posts, etc are all now retargetable.

This is the modern day way to build email lists, but inside of the social media channels for 100X-1000X cheaper while also being able to build hundreds of thousands into a retargetable list in days, and at scale in hours compared to the weeks or months it would take to convert that many people into your email list.

And unlike email marketing, your open rates are going to be near 100% because you'll be right in their newsfeed. Currently 53% of all attention on the internet goes to social media platforms, and 83% of those consuming content online are doing it with videos.

In addition, you are remaining omnipresent and contextual with your messaging in ads throughout the Harvester ad strategy, therefore you'll have an increased probability of converting those you're targeting because you're respecting them in the sales process, framing them with content, and staying in front of them guiding them to the next step in the sales cycle where they left off."

The content is intended to establish curiosity, authority and social proof in under 60 second.

### Testing & Audience Building

"I recommend testing 2-3 different video 1's that can be used for targeting your ideal audiences that you want to later convert into customers. Use engagement campaigns and video view campaigns optimized for thruplay and only charging you when a thruplay occurs. Thruplays are when someone reaches 15 seconds in a video.

The reason you're using both thruplay and engagement campaigns is because the algorithm is going to reach two different types of people in the same audience you're targeting in them based on the people in the audience are more likely to do to achieve your objective based on their previous app experience.

Be sure to set up audiences for what we call warm audiences, which are website traffic, social interaction on Facebook and Instagram, and any custom audiences like email lists you want to upload. Set up these audiences with different date ranges, like 5 days, 10 days, and every 5 day increments up to 365 days.

- This will be tedious, but worth it.
- Leverage this tool from Adrian Morrison to create the audiences faster and in bulk: https://audaccelerator.crmsecureorders.com/orderformtrial"

### Cycle Bin 1: Warming Cold Audiences

"Now that you've got your video 1 set up, you're going to want to drive your warm audience to what we're going to call Cycle Bin 1.

'Cycle Bin' refers to a group of videos that we are going to put in front of specific audiences that frame them, shape their beliefs and viewpoints, and increase our probability that they will respond to our offers and direct response ads.

For cycle bin 1, we need to make this newly added, and recent cold audience group of people warmed up to the brand with the right content. We're looking to establish more authority bias, social proof and shape their beliefs over the next 3-5 days before showing them our direct response ads. The goal KPI should be a frequency of 3-5 in these 3-5 days of showing them content.

You can put as many videos as you want in Content Bin 1 campaign because the AI is going to target the warm audience with videos they are most likely to engage with or watch based on their previous interactions on the social platforms and website interactions they track.

So moral of the story, the audience won't see all 20 videos if that's how many you have in Content Cycle Bin 1, but they will see the right 3-5 they will most likely respond to if you hit the goal KPI for frequency.

The audience you're targeting with Content Cycle Bin 1 is the warm audience that is 3-5 days big. Use the shorter amount of days if you want to crunch the sales cycle or are on a thinner budget. Leverage the Facebook interactions audiences, Instagram interactions audiences, video view audiences, and anything else that represents someone who was cold and became warm in the last 3-5 days."

### Direct Response Ads (Days 5-30)

"Now get your direct response ads set up, driving people to your lead magnet, or in this case a webinar selling a high ticket product.

Be sure to have your retargeting ads also set up.

Best practices are to have at least 5-10 variations for each placement built out for your direct response ads for both warm traffic and your retargeting ads. This means build out creative for right side column, Facebook newsfeed, Instagram newsfeed, story placements across Facebook/Instagram/Messenger, Messenger placements, in stream ads, the marketplace, and the audience network.

Your audience for this step is going to be your warm audience of days 5-30.

As you're targeting them with direct response ads now driving them to your webinar, you'll also want to be targeting that same audience with Content Cycle Bin 2.

Follow the same logic as Content Cycle Bin 1, meaning you can use up to 20-25 different videos that help shape their beliefs and manage their perceptions to frame them to view you the best way to have the highest probability to convert them now that they are being targeted with ads and are going to see your high ticket offer."

### Post-Opt-In: Trust Funnel (2-Day Email/Messenger Sequence)

"Once someone responds by opting into your webinar or lead magnet, be sure to have a Facebook Messenger opt in from a tool like Manychat.com to enable follow up in email and in messenger. Have marketing automation built out for both.

Leverage a 2 day trust funnel of follow up that sends them videos that are anywhere from 10-20 minutes long with additional value fortifying you're an expert at what you're teaching. An example is Brad Lea leveraging these videos in his trust funnel to solidify his authority by giving value to sales people:

- https://www.facebook.com/TheRealBradLea/videos/355664738417393/
- https://www.facebook.com/TheRealBradLea/videos/370772720190864/
- https://www.facebook.com/TheRealBradLea/videos/402085430592566/

The trust funnel videos can be sent via email and messenger. The videos can contain a CTA to get the main offer being pushed lightly at the end of each video.

If you're leveraging a CTA to schedule a call (which historically converts at much higher closing rates than online sales), be sure to pepper in throughout the video a few times to schedule a call by filling out the application below if the videos will be hosted on a landing page and not posted as regular videos on Facebook.

Ideally you're leveraging a webinar funnel where the just in time feature or on demand feature is on so they don't have to schedule at a later date, as attendance rate will increase significantly if on demand is an option. Always test to be sure this is true for your model and audience."

### Webinar Retargeting By Time Spent

"Have audiences set up through https://connectio.io/connectretarget/ to be able to retarget people based on the time they spent on the page. That way you can have audiences set up to enable you contextually speak to the person based on where they stopped in the webinar process.

Examples include:

- **On page for less than 30 seconds:** Follow up via retargeting with creative that calls them out on not staying to watch, hit them with a few different angles on what you cover and drive them back to the live room.

- **On page for less than 2 min, 5 min, 10 min, etc:** for whatever you contextually want to follow up with.

- **Don't be lazy with this:** This is a massive step that can open a lot more revenue because the more people that watch your webinar, the more revenue you'll likely generate.

- **Extended time tracking:** This can also be done on page for more than 60 min, 90 min, and those ads can drive them to the sales page."

### Sales Page Retargeting: Multiple Buying Motives

"Once someone watches the webinar or the webinar replay, they need to buy.

People buy for different reasons when there is more than one reason to buy.

When I ask people why they buy protein powder, it's generally for one or two reasons. To recover, and to get bigger.

When I ask people why they want to buy a lamborghini, I often hear up to 25 reasons, that range from all types of desires and biases.

My point being is that protein power companies have it made easy, they can have simple tag lines and call to actions - like 'recover and get bigger' because that's really all anyone is buying it for anyway, which simplifies their marketing.

Think of Lamborghini though, if they simplified their marketing down to 'go fast' they would 24 other types of buyers who have completely different buying motives like buying for status, or to prove to themselves they could do it, or to show off, or to prove others wrong, etc.

Point being you are most likely in the lamborghini situation, where you need to map out the 20+ different reasons someones actually going to buy your product. And this is very important, really think on this and then execute on creating ads for all placements that you use in a retageting sequence."

### Retargeting Sequence: 5-7 Day Intervals Over 180 Days

"The retargeting sequence is a sequence of ads optimized for all placements on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger that are audiences that visited the sales page and didn't buy with 5-7 day intervals in between the audiences being targeted with each retagreting ad in your sequence until they get targeted with the ad that makes them convert.

That means you're going to have ad sets that are spaced 5-7 days apart from one another with 20+ different creatives that are using 20+ different hooks and angles to attempt to sell the person who went to your sales page and didn't buy. Do the same in email and messenger follow up sequences.

And yes, this retargeting campaign should go for a few weeks up to 180 days, and the reason it's capped at 180 days of leveraging different hooks and angles to attempt to convert your warmest audience, is because that's when FB limits our ability to target past.

Now that you've converted your audience into customers, let's treat them like customers for a few days to maximize the usage of your product, and the strengthen their relationship with your brand all while increasing the success of them getting results with your product.

These videos and messages should be centered around product demos, customer successes, best practices to get the most out of the product or upcoming information, etc.

Target your thank you page website traffic for a 3-5 day duration and solely treat them like a customer, don't try to upsell them yet as this process is going to increase your chances of converting them and will also lower attrition rate on recurring products if you execute this step."

### Content Cycle Bin 3: Long-Term Customer Retention (Days 5-180)

"After you've treated them like a customer, this person is going to be supercharged to interact with your brand. Be sure to keep your brand omnipresent in front of this person. The audience now being targeted with Content Cycle Bin 3 is thank you page website visitors for days 5-180 which you can do by having an audience created for 180 days and excluding an audience of days 1-5.

The content is this specific content cycle bin since they are now a customer is still intended to frame them to view us and the world right way. We just need to keep re-enforcing this new reality they just entered. This a great time to put content in front of them that has your brands values, strong messaging, and core pieces of knowledge they need to have a high probability of buying the products that will be sold to them next.

As an example, if you have a recurring inner circle product, you could feed them content in Content Cycle Bin 3 that relates to how valuable more advanced lessons have been and how much faster you've been able to generate results being closers to the source of the data, and show these lessons being taught where people are in a mastermind setting learning from you but don't pitch the mastermind yet.

A good time to upsell is anywhere from 15 days to 45 days away from the initial purchase depending on the product type.

The harvester is ever green once you set it up, so set it up and enjoy reaping the customers in a healthy harvesting cycle again and again and again. Always be testing!"
