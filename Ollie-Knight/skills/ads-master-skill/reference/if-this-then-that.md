## Problem: Low Conversions

### Step-by-Step Diagnosis & Solution

1. The first step if this happens is to find the bottleneck in the funnel.

2. This requires looking at the ad data like CTR and total click volume, and then looking at each step of data for all steps throughout the funnel.
   - Good CTR is above 2%

3. Ideally you're using clickfunnels so you can see the data in the dashboard easily for each step of the funnel.

4. **Funnel Example Breakdown:**

   a. Let's say you have 1,000 people that hit your opt in page, and 100 opted in, that's a 10% conversion rate for that step, 100 divided by 1000 to get 10%.

   b. Let's say this is a webinar funnel, and that the webinar page has 98 people that hit after opting in, and only 12 get to the order page of the funnel. That means there is only 12% of the people who opt in for the webinar, hitting the order page - 12 divided by 98 to get 12.2%.

   c. Let's say out of those 12, 2 people purchase. That means there is a 16% conversion rate from order form to purchase, 2 divided by 12 to get 16%.

5. From there you can also factor in any up-sells and their conversion rates.

6. Once done, you'll get a perspective on where the choke point of the funnel is.

7. And as a marketer your objective should be to put attention on wherever the choke point is to increase conversion throughout the rest of the funnel, and then repeat this until the desired KPI conversion rates are met throughout the entire process of ads to end step in the funnel.

## Problem: CTR is less than 2%

### Immediate Action Steps

1. If you're click through rate on a given funnel is too low, sub 2%, then this problem is relatively simple to solve.

2. You need to rotate in new creatives on an every 72 hour basis until the CTR is higher than 2%.

3. You could rotate in new headlines, new body copy, new images or videos, you could leverage dynamic creative vs static creative - essentially any changes are good changes when you're below 2% CTR and in the testing phase of an ad.

   a. One important caveat to mention, is that if you're ads are profitable and still below sub 2% CTR, there is no need to change the creatives in that given campaign, it'd be best to duplicate that campaign, test new audiences with the same creative to see if it will also be profitable or have a higher CTR than 2%.

   i. **Never turn something off that is profitable.**

4. Some tools for clients that have limited availability or suck at making new image or videos to leverage, are tools like shakr.com or tools like canva.com to easily create video and image variations without needing the client to create new assets if there's anything that holds that process back.

## Problem: Learning Limited

### Understanding the Learning Limited Phase

1. If you enter the learning limited phase of an ad set, it is Facebook or Adwords indicating to you that you do not have a sufficient amount of conversions for whatever you're optimizing your campaigns for.

2. What then happens is the algorithms don't know who to confidently go after out of everyone you're targeting because there's not enough data to reinforce the machine learning model that underlays the advertising platforms.

3. There's a few processes you could implement, some longer than others and some simpler than others.

### Solution: Process One - Relaunch the Ad Set

4. Process one would be to simply relaunch that ad set if you're optimizing for ad set budget optimization IF you got close to the numbers of conversions in a week you need to hit to exit said learning phase.

   a. This gives a high probability that you will exit since you got close upon the initial launch of the campaign, but missed by only a few conversions.

### Solution: Process Two - Fix the Funnel Bottleneck

5. If you were way short of the number you needed to hit to exit the learning phase, then you need to consider going over the first problem in this SOP, which is to find the bottleneck throughout your funnel and fix it - and then intend to relaunch after as another test.

### Solution: Process Three - Test New Audiences

6. If you're not seeing any results after a few pivots in your funnel in an attempt to increase your conversions overall in the funnel itself, another good solution could be to test new audiences with the best performing creatives out of everything tested so far.

   a. This will give you a clear indication of whether you're not seeing enough conversions to exit the learning phase due to the audience attempting to be targeted not responding to the creatives or funnel type you're marketing to them.

### Solution: Process Four - Run Cyclic Campaigns

7. Another great solution to exiting the learning phase is to run cyclic campaigns.

   a. There is a training on this in DSP - Digital Skill Pack and also in the high ticket agency course in the social media advertising module.

   b. The intention of a cyclic campaign is to run campaigns optimized for each step of the funnel leveraging a different standard event relative to the step of the funnel.

   i. **Ecommerce Store Example:** Optimize an ecom store funnel for purchase, then duplicate that campaign and optimize for the step above it as well like add to cart or initiate checkout, then duplicate that campaign and optimize for view content or whatever standard event is above that 2nd to last step in your funnel flow.

   ii. **Application Funnel Example:** In an application funnel campaign, you could optimize for the furthest down the funnel step being complete registration or schedule call, and then duplicate the campaign and optimize for submit application, then duplicate it out again and optimize for lead.

   iii. **Benefit:** This will ultimately help you get more data throughout all steps of the funnel, and will help you exit the learning phase faster so you can get more reach and a higher volume of cost effective conversions for whatever you're attempting to advertise.

8. **Pro tip with the learning phase:** Understand machine learning and WHY Facebook or Adwords is indicating this to you, so you can understand the importance of why to properly exit leveraging these tactics above.

## Problem: CPA cost is too high

### Diagnosis & Multi-Layered Solutions

1. When your cost per action is too high for whatever you're optimizing your campaigns for, there are a lot of variables that could be causing this issue, we will go through them in this step together for troubleshooting.

### Issue 1: Audience Size Too Small

2. First is the audiences being targeted are too small, and you're spending too much to target them with automated bidding.

   a. This problem will happen for audiences sizing less than 1,000,000 people.

   i. **Easiest fix:** Widen the amount of people being targeted.

   ii. **If that's not possible:** Consider dropping the daily budget 20% until CPA is lower and then scaling back up again 10-20% daily until desired CPA is conditioned.

   iii. **Alternative approach:** Run a manual bid campaign and see if you get reach at the desired CPA you're attempting to hit with the small audience to see if it's even possible to achieve at the rate you're looking for conversions at.

### Issue 2: Hyper-Targeted Demographic (High Competition)

3. CPA can also be high if you're targeting a hyper targeted demographic.

   a. **Historical example:** In Q4 of 2020 there was a tense election where swing voters and swing states were being targeted aggressively, and Covid-19 happened that year which caused for a HUGE increase of ecommerce spend in Q4 and retailers who got fucked all year not being able to convert their usual customers in stores relying on the Q4 season to make up for that revenue.

   i. **Solution Strategy:** We switched to manual bid campaigns, and would run a campaign at $200/day as an example budget with a $50/bid cap, and every 2 hours I'd monitor reach of those campaigns.

   ii. **If reach wasn't ideal:** We'd bump the bid cap $10 every 2 hours until we started getting reach.

   iii. **Then scale spending:** Increase the daily spend by whatever each cost per conversion was. So if our daily budget was $200/day and we got the first conversion at $40, our new daily budget would get bumped to $240, etc for each additional conversion.

   iv. **Audience saturation management:** Once the reach stopped or got throttled again, we knew that we cleared out everyone that day available in the bid cap range and we'd bump it another $10/bid cap to clear out the next range of higher cost customers.

   v. **Benefit:** This strategy works for any hyper competitive demographics you might be targeting that you are intending to scale with a high volume of consumers, but you want to maintain an ideal CPA. It also helps to know the exact amount you're going to need to spend per conversion, so you can make adjustments on the backend to pricing so you're still profitable.

   vi. **Additional note:** It's important to note for this strategy that you are going to want to ensure you're duplicating campaigns and testing new audiences, ideally broad audiences so you don't run out of ideal CPA buyers in a given range before having to bump to a bid cap above your desired KPI.

### Issue 3: Scaling Mistakes

4. CPAs can also jump through the roof if you scale a campaign wrong.

   a. Any campaign that leverages automated bidding and is scaled more than 10-30% in a given daily budget increase is susceptible to drastic increases in CPA costs.

   b. **Solution:** The solution if you want to scale a campaign from let's say $200/day to $1,000/day is to duplicate out the $200/day campaign and set the budget on the newly created campaign to $1,000/day, once approved you shut off the $200/day campaign so you avoid audience overlap.

### Issue 4: Audience Testing

5. Also important to test new audiences to see if a given audience you're attempting to target is a higher CPA than another one being tested.

## Problem: High no show rate on scheduled calls

### Benchmark Standards & Solutions

1. 20% or less no show rate is good, 30% is ok, anything above 35% is unacceptable and costing you or your clients big money, and most marketers don't increase their cost per scheduled call by adding the inflated cost of no shows to it.

### Solution 1: Audit & Improve Marketing Automation

2. The best actions for decreasing no show rates start by auditing the marketing automation.

   a. In most cases, marketing automation is left generic with basic reminders.

   b. This is a great part of the follow up pre call sequence that can be used to pepper in some testimonials and to treat these emails as a resell to why they need to show up each reminder that's sent.

### Solution 2: Leverage Text-Based Reminders

3. Be sure to leverage text based reminders as well.

   a. Add an option to collect phone number in the scheduler if you're not already doing this anyway.

### Solution 3: Video Text from the Closer

4. Send a video text IMMEDIATELY after an application OR scheduled call comes in from the closer.

   a. This is a real, custom video text that gets sent to the applicant or scheduled caller that implies they are excited to chat, wanted to face to the person they'll be speaking with that's going to help them, and to send any questions over between now and the call time.

   i. **Best practice:** Best sent from iPhones, as iPhones don't reduce the quality of the video text.

   ii. **Alternative:** Can also be sent over DMs to the persons social media if the closer doesn't have an iPhone so there's no excuses not to send a video message.

### Solution 4: Retargeting Campaign

5. Run a retargeting campaign with a contextual set of content that directly states "were excited to help now that you've scheduled a call with us, here's something you can do/become aware of/some things to expect on the call together" type piece of content.

   a. This is a low budget daily spend campaign that takes $5-$10 that increases the overall effectiveness of each scheduled caller before they show up.

   b. Needs to be an audience of at least 100 to work, so really only effective if you have more than 100 callers in a given period of time between scheduled and the call itself for this audience.

### Solution 5: Confirmation Page with VSL

6. Another important step in decreasing the no show rate is to have a final step in the funnel that acts as a confirmation step.

   a. This should have VSL that explainers what to expect on the call and congratulating them on getting a step closer to solving or dealing with X.

   b. Also a great page to add testimonials to for further framing to get the person more committed to showing up for the call.

### Combined Results

7. All of this in combination generally keeps the no shows below 20%.

## Problem: A lot of add to carts, but few sales

### E-Commerce Conversion Optimization Tactics

1. Typically most order forms or check out pages lack general best practices for conversion rate optimization, this will act as a list of what to do so you get some ideas of actions to implement to get more people buying.

### Tactic 1: Dynamic Product Retargeting

2. If you're running a Shopify store, or a site with another Facebook or google marketing partner like marketo or woocommerce, you can set up a catalog feed that takes all the products and products categories and allows you to run "dynamic product retargeting ads".

   a. This shows people who went and viewed a product, or added to cart and didn't buy, or those who bought and can be cross sold a set of products that are within the same product category or similar to the product category of what they previously browsed, added to cart or bought to bring them back to the store with the highest probability.

### Tactic 2: Green Call to Action Button

3. You can leverage green call to action buttons for higher conversion rates on order forms and closing pages.

### Tactic 3: Support & Trust Signals

4. Add support options like email and phone number on page for additional trust.

   a. Adding an address to the site has also been showed to increase user trust.

### Tactic 4: Testimonials (Tweet Style)

5. Adding easy to read testimonials that are short and formatted like tweets help conversions.

### Tactic 5: FAQ Section

6. Having an FAQ section acts as a digital objection handling section where you can address the objections customers may have in a list to increase conversions.

### Tactic 6: Product Visualization

7. Visualizing products or adding influencer/customer unboxing videos can help with conversions.

### Tactic 7: Live Chat

8. Adding a live chat feature in bottom right corner helps.

### Tactic 8: Shipping Information

9. Adding clear shipping times or doing free shipping typically helps with conversions for physical products.

### Tactic 9: Social Proof Tools

10. Using tools like useproof.com or enabling "digital table rush" on Clickfunnels can increase conversions as it shows everyone else who is buying with a small pop up in the bottom left corner, you can leverage total quantity of site visitors with useproof.com which can be great social proof for high traffic stores.

### Tactic 10: Trust Icons

11. Adding trust icons like this can help: [reference to trust badge imagery]

### Combined Impact

12. All of these can add single digit increases in conversion % rates, in combination with one another these are hyper effective.

## Problem: Leads aren't coming in framed properly

### Complete Funnel Audit Process

1. This is where you need to look at your campaign entirely to audit where something could be off or whats completely missing.

   a. Best to go through it like a customer you want to sell.

   b. Start with the ads, work your way into the funnel watch the videos if any, and schedule out a call or call the business if there's no scheduler like secrets shoppers would do at retailers. Find what's off in the marketing automation, and noted everything.

   i. **You're looking to get the customer experience first hand, and then to reflect on that experience as a marketer to see what can be improved.**

   ii. **The most common areas are:** The ads, funnel, marketing automation and sales conversation happening.

   1. **Also little things:** Like no VSLs (video sales letter) on the pages, this makes huge difference.

   2. **Also the difference:** Between generic reminder marketing automation vs well written copy in the follow up and reminder sequences.

### Solution: Incorporate Framing Content Throughout Campaign

2. Another big campaign shift that can make all the difference on the ad side is the incorporation of framing content throughout the ad campaigns.

   a. This is big part of ad strategies I've created like the Venus Fly Trap™ and The Harvester™ ad strategies.

   b. These strategies incorporate content at all stages of the ad strategy and are attached here below.

   c. You'll see within these ad strategies how content can be used for framing initially before the prospect even sees the direct response creatives, and you'll see how content can be leveraged throughout all stages of the funnel for proper framing that's contextual and beneficial for each step of the process.
