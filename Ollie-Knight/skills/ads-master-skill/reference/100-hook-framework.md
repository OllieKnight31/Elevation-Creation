# 100 Hook Framework (Organized by Cognitive Bias)

_Source: File 3 — Ad Diversification Mastery SOP. 100 hooks across 7 categories. Each labelled with its underlying cognitive bias and application. Use this AFTER the Dog Food Exercise has produced your reasons — pick a cognitive bias that matches the reason, then a hook from that category._

## Section: The 100 Hook Framework

### Overview
Organized into six categories based on psychological principles. Each category targets different cognitive biases and emotional states.

## Section: Hook Usage Guidelines

### For Maximum Effectiveness

1. **Test Multiple Categories**: Don't stick to one type of hook
2. **Match Hook to Audience**: Consider where your audience is in their journey
3. **Combine with Strong Body Copy**: Hook gets attention, body copy converts
4. **A/B Test Variations**: Small changes can dramatically impact performance
5. **Track Performance by Category**: Identify which psychological triggers work best for your audience

### Key Meta-Insight

"The biggest constraint you can put on your Meta strategy is the constraints you place on your creative process."
